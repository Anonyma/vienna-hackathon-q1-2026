SEC.01 // MISSION BRIEF
001 — ABOUT THE MISSION

NOT JUST ANOTHER HACKATHON

OpenClaw for Impact gathers the top builders, researchers, and clinicians who believe technology should serve a purpose beyond profit. This is where AI meets medicine, where security meets healthcare, where code becomes a force for measurable good.

No pitch decks. No investor panels. Just 24 hours of raw building — shipping real prototypes that could change how we diagnose, treat, and protect patients. Whether you're a seasoned developer or picking up your first API, our workshops and mentors will get you shipping.

Newcomers welcome. We run dedicated workshop tracks so everyone can contribute — from first-time hackers to published researchers.

SEC.04 // PLATFORM
004 — THE PLATFORM

WHAT IS OPENCLAW

OpenClaw is an open-source initiative building tools at the intersection of AI and impact. We believe the most important technology should be free, auditable, and community-driven — especially when lives are at stake.

The platform provides frameworks for building AI-powered healthcare applications — from clinical decision support systems to secure data pipelines. Every project at this hackathon contributes back to the OpenClaw ecosystem, creating a growing library of open-source tools that anyone can deploy.

Our mission is simple: make the tools that matter accessible to everyone. No paywalls. No lock-in. Just code that works, reviewed by clinicians, built by builders, deployed where it's needed most.
