SEC.01 // MISSION BRIEF
001 — ABOUT THE MISSION

OpenClaw Hack_001 is Vienna's first overnight agent hackathon — a 24-hour build sprint where AI agents meet real-world problems.

No pitch decks. No investor panels. Just 24 hours of raw building — shipping agents, tools, and prototypes that actually work. Bring your own stack, your own model, your own ideas. We provide the space, the mentors, and the infrastructure.

Newcomers welcome — we run workshop tracks for all levels.

SEC.04 // PLATFORM
004 — THE PLATFORM

WHAT IS OPENCLAW

OpenClaw is an open-source initiative building tools at the intersection of AI agents and real-world impact. The project believes the most powerful technology should be free, auditable, and community-driven.

The mission is simple: make agent infrastructure accessible to everyone. No paywalls. No lock-in. Just code that works, built by builders, deployed where it's needed most.
